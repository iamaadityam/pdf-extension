PDF Notes Chrome Extension

Quick Setup Guide — Ready in under 1 minute

🚀 3 Easy Steps to Install

1
Open Chrome Extensions Page

Open a new tab in Google Chrome (or Brave) and navigate to the extensions page:

chrome://extensions
2
Turn on "Developer mode"

In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, switch the toggle for Developer mode to ON.

3
Click "Load unpacked" & Select Folder

Click the Load unpacked button in the top-left and select this extracted folder. That's all!

💡 How to Use PDF Notes

🖍️
1. Highlight & Right-Click

Select any sentence in any online or local PDF, right-click, and choose "Save to PDF Notes".

📖
2. Side Panel Companion

The side panel opens beside your PDF. Label notes as Key Ideas, Quotes, Definitions, or Questions with emojis.

✏️
3. Add Custom Notes

Click the + Note button in the side panel to jot down your own personal thoughts or reminders.

📥
4. One-Click Export

Export all your notes into Microsoft Word (.doc), Markdown (.md) for Notion, or print-ready format with one click.
